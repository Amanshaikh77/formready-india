Privacy Policy

Last Modified: September 2026

At FormReady India (accessible from formready-india.onrender.com), the security, anonymity, and digital confidentiality of our visitors are fundamental design principles. This Privacy Policy document details the types of information we do not collect, how local processing works, and our regulatory disclosures regarding third-party service providers.

1. The Zero-Storage Data Policy (Client-Side Guarantee)

Our core operational model is built entirely around browser-level execution. When you utilize any tool on FormReady India—including the AI Background Remover, ID Card to A4 Maker, Sarkari PDF Compressor, or Signature Joiner:

  • No File Transmission: Your uploaded documents, identity credentials, certificates, photographs, and signatures are processed inside your device's browser memory (via WebAssembly & HTML5 Canvas).
  • No Database Archiving: We maintain zero server-side databases, file repositories, or cloud buckets. Files are wiped from memory as soon as you close or refresh the web page.
  • No Biometric Retention: We do not log facial dimensions, biometric data, or identity numbers from Aadhaar or PAN documents.

2. Log Files and Web Analytics

Like standard web infrastructure, our hosting environment captures standard HTTP web access logs for security and performance diagnostics. These logs include IP addresses, browser variants, referring pages, and operating timestamps. These system metrics contain no personally identifiable records and are used exclusively to monitor uptime.

3. Cookies and Advertising Partners (Google AdSense)

FormReady India utilizes third-party advertising partners, notably Google AdSense, to sustain continuous server maintenance and free utility access:

  • Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick DART cookie) to serve ads to users based on visits to FormReady India and other websites across the web.
  • Users may choose to decline personalized advertising by navigating to the Google Ads Settings page.
