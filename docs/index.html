How Our Tools Work

Instant processing directly in your browser without waiting for server queues.

1

Image Processing Tools

Tools like Photo Cropper, Resizer, and Compressor read your images through your browser’s HTML5 Canvas. Adjustments and quality compression run on your device's memory, giving immediate downloads without uploading a single pixel.

2

PDF Tools (Merge & Split)

Built using client-side WebAssembly and pdf-lib, our PDF tools parse document structures, rearrange pages, and compile new files in your device RAM. Your private certificates and identity proofs stay strictly offline.

3

Form Utilities

The Signature Maker, Passport Photo generator, and Photo Sheet tool apply predefined government standard ratios locally. Once you preview and tap download, files are saved directly into your device storage.

🛡️

Zero Server Uploads · 100% Private Local Processing

Unlike other converters, FormReady India does not upload your Aadhaar, PAN, certificates, or personal photos to external servers or databases. All file processing, cropping, and PDF manipulation happens locally inside your phone/computer browser. Your personal data never leaves your device.
