Split PDF

Extract selected pages from a PDF and download them as a new PDF.

How this works
1
Choose your PDF file — its total page count will show up automatically.
2
Set the From Page and To Page you want — or drag the slider.
3
Tap Split PDF — a new PDF with just those pages downloads automatically.

Choose PDF

Select your PDF file

Example: If your PDF has 10 pages and you enter From Page 3 and To Page 7, the downloaded PDF will contain pages 3–7 (5 pages total).
