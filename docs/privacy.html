Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At FormReady India, we prioritize the privacy and security of job aspirants and document editors across India.

Zero Server-Side Storage

All photo cropping, background removal, PDF conversions, and document modifications happen 100% inside your browser using client-side JavaScript. No files, signatures, or biometric ID documents are ever uploaded to any cloud server.

Cookies and Advertising

We use standard third-party advertising partners, including Google AdSense, which may use cookies to serve ads based on non-identifiable visits to this and other websites.
